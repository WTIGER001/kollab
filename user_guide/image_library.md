# Image Library

The Image Library is a centralized place where you can upload and manage images that you want to reuse across multiple pages and documents in Kollab.

## Finding the Image Library
You can access your image library from the **Sidebar Navigation**.
1. Click the `...` menu next to the name of your current workspace (e.g. your Team name, Project name, or Personal Space).
2. Click **Image Library** from the dropdown menu.

## How Scopes Work
The Image Library is context-aware based on where you open it from:
- **Personal Library**: Images uploaded here are private to you.
- **Project Library**: Images uploaded here are available to anyone working within the current Project.
- **Team Library**: Images uploaded here are available to anyone working within the entire Team.
- **System Library**: A global repository for company-wide assets (like official logos) managed by administrators.

## Managing Images
Once inside the library, you can:
- **Upload**: Click the `Upload Image` button in the top right corner to select a file from your computer.
- **Rename**: Click the pencil icon next to an image to give it a friendly, recognizable name.
- **Delete**: Click the trash can icon to delete an image. *(Warning: Deleting an image will break it on any document where it is currently being used!)*

Images uploaded to the library can be quickly accessed when inserting Hero Banners or standard images into your documents.
