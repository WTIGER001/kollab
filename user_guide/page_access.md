# Page access

Kollab follows the familiar Confluence approach: access normally comes from the space, while a page restriction is used for exceptions.

Open a page, choose **Restrictions**, and use **General access** to decide whether people with parent or space access can view the page. Turn it off to make the page visible only to people and groups listed under **Specific access**.

Specific access can be **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**. A view restriction on a parent page also applies to its child pages. Editing a parent does not automatically give someone edit access to every child.

Being listed as a member of a space does not, by itself, grant access. A space administrator assigns a space role, and page restrictions refine that role when needed.

## Share a page by link

Open **Link Sharing** from the page toolbar. If you can manage sharing, create a link with a Viewer, Commenter, or Editor role, and optionally set a password and expiration. Copy its `/share/…` address. Visitors enter the password when required; anonymous visitors can read, while commenting and editing require sign-in and a role that permits the action.

Revoking a link or letting it expire stops it from granting access, including to protected media. A page's sharing link does not grant general access to unrelated workspace content.
