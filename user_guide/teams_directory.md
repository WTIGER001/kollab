# Teams Directory

The Teams Directory is your central hub for exploring the organization's collaborative spaces. You can access it by navigating to `/teams` or by selecting "Teams Directory" from the main navigation (if available).

## Exploring Teams

When you open the directory, you will see a grid of all available collaborative **Teams** in the organization. 
- Your **Personal Space** is not listed here, as this directory focuses strictly on shared team environments.
- Each team card displays the team's name, abbreviation (slug), and a brief description of its purpose.

## Accessing Projects

Underneath the team description, you'll see a list of **Projects** associated with that team.
- **Quick Access**: You can click directly on a project chip (e.g., `Kollab Test`) to bypass the main team portal and jump straight into the project's workspace.
- **Team Portal**: Clicking anywhere else on the team card (or the "Open Portal" button) will take you to the main Team Portal, where you can view all projects and team members in more detail.

## Visual Themes
The Teams Directory fully supports the dynamic Theme Engine. The cards, background gradients, and hover animations will seamlessly adapt to whichever Theme Preset (e.g., Neobrutal, Editorial, Workbench) and Mode (Light/Dark) you have currently selected.
