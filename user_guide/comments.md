# User Guide: Document Comments & Nested Threads

Collaborative comments allow team members to discuss document content, ask questions, leave feedback, and thread discussions directly inline with the text.

---

## 1. Viewing Comments

Comments are anchored directly to specific text in the document.
- Text with a comment is highlighted in yellow.
- Clicking on highlighted text opens the **Comments Drawer** on the right side of the screen.
- The drawer displays all comments and nested replies associated with that specific text highlight.
- Each comment is labeled with the author's color-coded **User Initials Avatar** (which extracts the first letter of each word in their display name up to 3 initials).

---

## 2. Posting Comments & Replies

### Adding a New Inline Comment
1. Highlight any text in the document.
2. A small **Add Comment** icon will appear near your selection.
3. Click the icon to anchor a new comment to the text.
4. The Comments Drawer will slide out. Type your comment and click the **Send** button.

### Replying to an Existing Comment
1. Click on an existing text highlight in the document to open the Comments Drawer.
2. At the bottom of the drawer, type your reply into the text box.
3. Click **Send** or press `Enter` to post your reply.

---

## 3. Editing and Deleting Your Comments

If you are the author of a comment, you can delete it:
1. Open the Comments Drawer by clicking the highlighted text.
2. Hover over your comment to reveal the **Trash Bin** icon on the right.
3. Click the Trash Bin icon to delete the comment.

*Note:* If you delete the *last* comment in a thread, the yellow highlight in the document will automatically disappear.

---

## 4. Hiding / Showing Comments

If you want to read a document without being distracted by yellow comment highlights, you can easily hide them:
1. Locate the **Hide/Show Comments** toggle button (a small chat bubble icon) on the top right toolbar of the editor.
2. Click it to toggle the visibility of comment highlights in the text.
3. When toggled off, all highlights vanish, but your comments are safely preserved in the background!

---

## ⚠️ Comment Restrictions

- **Read-Only / Deleted Pages**: If a page is inside the Trash Bin, the comments section is set to read-only. You can read existing comments, but posting new comments and replies is disabled.
