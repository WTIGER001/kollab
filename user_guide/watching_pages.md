# Watching Pages

Use page watching when you want to keep track of a document without adding it to your Favorites.

## Watch or unwatch a page

1. Open the page you want to follow.
2. In the upper-right toolbar, select the Bell icon.
3. A filled Bell means you are watching that page. Select it again to stop watching.

Your choice is saved to your account, so it is available on another device after you sign in.

## What watching does today

Watching records your subscription to that specific page. It does not yet create an in-app notification, email, or a project-wide/team-wide inherited watch. Those delivery and parent-space features will be introduced in a later update.

Favorites and watches are independent: use the Star for quick access and the Bell for a saved subscription.
