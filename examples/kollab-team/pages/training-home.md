# Training & Workshops

This project supports a 60–75 minute introduction for authors, reviewers, and administrators. Use the PowerPoint to introduce each concept, then switch to the matching page for a live demonstration. Participants can complete the workshops in a disposable copy of this team.

## Course plan

| Segment | Suggested time | Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation | 10 minutes | Locate a team, project, and page; try search |
| Authoring and macros | 20 minutes | Edit a callout, status, task, table, and diagram |
| Collaboration and governance | 15 minutes | Comment, review, and inspect history |
| Recovery and administration | 10 minutes | Distinguish version restore, backup restore, and sync |
| Guided practice and questions | 15 minutes | Complete the workshop checklist |

These are suggested teaching durations, not measured product performance.
